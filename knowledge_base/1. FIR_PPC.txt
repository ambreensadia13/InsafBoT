FIR AND PAKISTAN PENAL CODE - BASICS

1. FIR - First Information Report:
FIR police ki pehli report hoti hai. PPC Section 154 ke teht koi bhi shaksi bina fees ke FIR darj karwa sakta hai. Agar police FIR na likhe to SP ya Magistrate ko application di ja sakti hai.

2. PPC Section 302 - Qatl/Murder:
Jan boojh kar qatl ki saza death ya life imprisonment hai.

3. PPC Section 420 - Fraud/Dhokha:
Dhokay se property ya paisa lena. Saza 7 saal tak qaid aur jurmana.

4. PPC Section 376 - Zina bil jabr/Rape:
Iski saza death ya 10-25 saal qaid hai.

NEXT STEPS:
FIR ke liye qareebi police station jayen. Sath CNIC aur waqiye ki details le jayen.

SOURCE:
Pakistan Penal Code 1860, CrPC 1898
