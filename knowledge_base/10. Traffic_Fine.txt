TRAFFIC LAW - PUNJAB MOTOR VEHICLE RULES

1. License Zaroori:
Bina license driving par 5000 PKR tak jurmana aur gaari band ho sakti hai.

2. Helmet/Seatbelt:
Motorcycle par helmet aur car mein seatbelt na pehnne par 2000 PKR jurmana.

3. Over-speeding:
Speed limit torhne par 1000 se 5000 PKR tak challan.

4. Wrong Parking:
Ghalat jagah parking par gaari uthayi ja sakti hai + 500 PKR fine.

NEXT STEPS:
1. Challan City Traffic Police ki website se check karen.
2. Bank mein fees jama karwayen.

SOURCE:
Provincial Motor Vehicle Ordinance 1965
