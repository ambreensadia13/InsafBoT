CONSUMER PROTECTION - PUNJAB CONSUMER ACT

1. Nakis Cheez:
Agar product kharab ho ya service ghalat ho to dukandaar zimmedar hai.

2. Complaint Kahan:
Zila Consumer Court mein case file karen. Wakeel ki zaroorat nahi. Fees 100 PKR.

3. Proof Chahiye:
Bill, receipt, warranty card, product ki photo rakhen.

4. Compensation:
Court nuqsan ka azala + jurmana dilwa sakti hai.

NEXT STEPS:
1. Pehle shopkeeper ko written complaint den.
2. 30 din mein Consumer Court jayen.

SOURCE:
Punjab Consumer Protection Act 2005
