WORKPLACE HARASSMENT - PROTECTION ACT 2010

1. Kya Hai:
Office mein aurat ko tang karna, gandi baat, touch karna ya promotion ke liye pressure.

2. Inquiry Committee:
Har company mein 3 logon ki committee honi chahiye. 3 din mein shikayat likhni hai.

3. Saza:
Qasoorwar ko nokri se nikalna + 1 lakh tak jurmana.

4. Confidential:
Shikayat karne wali ka naam khufiya rakha jata hai.

NEXT STEPS:
1. HR ya Inquiry Committee ko application den.
2. Ombudsperson ko bhi complaint ho sakti hai.

SOURCE:
Protection Against Harassment of Women at Workplace Act 2010
