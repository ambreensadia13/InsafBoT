CRIMINAL LAW - BAIL PROCESS

1. Bailable Offence:
Choti FIR jaise 337, 427 mein police station se hi bail mil sakti hai.

2. Non-Bailable Offence:
Bari FIR jaise 302, 376 mein Magistrate/Session Court se bail leni parhti hai.

3. Bail Bond:
Court zamanat aur surety maangti hai. Wakeel bail application likhega.

4. Anticipatory Bail:
Agar arrest ka khatra ho to pehle se Session Court se bail li ja sakti hai.

NEXT STEPS:
1. Foran wakeel se rabta karen.
2. Court mein bail application file karen.

SOURCE:
Code of Criminal Procedure 1898
