INHERITANCE LAW - SUCCESSION PAKISTAN

1. Will/Wasiyat:
Muslim sirf 1/3 property ki wasiyat kar sakta hai. Baqi 2/3 waris mein qanoonan taqseem hogi.

2. Legal Heirs:
Beta, beti, biwi, walidain sab ka hissa Shariah ke mutabiq banta hai.

3. Succession Certificate:
Bank balance ya shares nikalwane ke liye Civil Court se certificate chahiye.

4. Mutation:
Patwari se zameen apne naam transfer karwane ko "inteqal" kehte hain.
