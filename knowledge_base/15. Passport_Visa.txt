PASSPORT AND VISA - BASIC GUIDE

1. New Passport:
NADRA CNIC + 2 photos + fees 4500 se 15000 PKR. Normal 10 din, urgent 5 din.

2. Lost Passport:
Pehle FIR, phir newspaper ad, phir passport office mein apply.

3. Visa Overstay:
Visa khatam hone ke baad rukne par fine aur blacklist ho sakta hai.

4. Complaint:
Passport ka masla ho to DG Immigration & Passports ko application den.

NEXT STEPS:
Qareebi Regional Passport Office jayen. Online appointment le len.

SOURCE:
Pakistan Passport Rules 1974
