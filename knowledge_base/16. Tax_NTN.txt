FBR TAX - NTN AND INCOME TAX

1. NTN Kya Hai:
National Tax Number har karobar aur job wale ke liye zaroori hai.

2. Tax Return:
Salana income 6 lakh se zyada ho to return file karna lazmi hai.

3. Filer vs Non-Filer:
Filer ko bank aur property par tax kam lagta hai. IRIS portal par register karen.

4. Penalty:
Return late file karne par 1000 PKR se 25000 PKR tak jurmana.

NEXT STEPS:
1. FBR IRIS website par account banayen.
2. Tax consultant se help len.

SOURCE:
Income Tax Ordinance 2001, FBR
