EDUCATION LAW - SCHOOL/COLLEGE FEE ISSUES

1. Illegal Fee Increase:
Punjab mein school saal mein 5% se zyada fee nahi barha sakta.

2. Complaint Kahan:
Punjab Education Department ya District Education Authority ko application den.

3. Admission Refusal:
Bina wajah admission se inkar ghalat hai. DC office mein shikayat ho sakti hai.

4. Scholarship:
Government ke ehsaas aur PEEF scholarship ke liye school se form len.

NEXT STEPS:
1. Principal ko written application.
2. DEO office mein complaint.

SOURCE:
Punjab Private Educational Institutions Ordinance
