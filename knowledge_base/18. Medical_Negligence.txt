MEDICAL LAW - DOCTOR NEGLIGENCE

1. Kya Hai:
Doctor ki ghalti se mareez ko nuqsan ho to ye medical negligence hai.

2. Complaint Kahan:
Pakistan Medical Commission PMC ko online complaint karen. Hospital admin ko bhi likhen.

3. Proof:
Medical report, prescription, test, bill sab rakhen.

4. Compensation:
Court hospital aur doctor se nuqsan ka azala dilwa sakti hai.

NEXT STEPS:
1. Hospital se medical record ki copy len.
2. PMC portal par complaint.

SOURCE:
Pakistan Medical and Dental Council Act
