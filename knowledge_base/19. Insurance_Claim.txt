INSURANCE LAW - CLAIM REJECTION

1. Claim Ka Haq:
Agar policy valid hai to company claim dene ki paband hai.

2. Rejection Ki Wajah:
Company 30 din mein wajah likh kar bataye gi.

3. Ombudsman:
Insurance Ombudsman free mein case sunta hai. 90 din ke andar apply karen.

4. Documents:
Policy, claim form, FIR, medical report, death certificate.

NEXT STEPS:
1. Company ko written appeal.
2. Federal Insurance Ombudsman ko complaint.

SOURCE:
Insurance Ordinance 2000
