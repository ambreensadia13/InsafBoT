TENANT AND LANDLORD LAW - PAKISTAN

1. Rent Agreement:
Zaban-i agreement bhi legal hai lekin written agreement behtar hai. Us mein rent, duration aur sharaait likhna zaroori hai.

2. Eviction - Ghar Khali Karwana:
Makan malik bina 30 din ka legal notice diye ghar khali nahi karwa sakta. Bijli/gas kaatna ya tala lagana illegal hai.

3. Rent Increase:
Makan malik har saal 10% se zyada rent nahi barha sakta agar agreement mein kuch aur na likha ho.

4. Tenant Rights:
Tenant ko safe aur rehne ke qabil ghar ka haq hai. Zaruri repair malik ki zimmedari hai.

2. Police ko 15 par call karen agar zabardasti ho.
