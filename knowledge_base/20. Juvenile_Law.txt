JUVENILE JUSTICE SYSTEM ACT 2018

1. Age Limit:
18 saal se kam umar bacha "juvenile" hai. Uska case alag court mein chalega.

2. Arrest Rule:
Police juvenile ko hathkari nahi laga sakti aur 24 ghante mein court mein pesh karegi.

3. Bail:
Juvenile ko asani se bail mil jati hai. Jail ki bajaye "Observation Home" bheja jata hai.

4. Record:
18 saal ke baad juvenile ka criminal record khatam kar diya jata hai.

NEXT STEPS:
Foran wakeel aur walidain ko itla den. Juvenile Court mein case chalega.
