RIGHT TO INFORMATION - RTI LAW

1. Kya Hai:
Har shehri sarkari daftar se koi bhi maloomat maang sakta hai.

2. Kese:
Daftar ke "PIO" ko 100 PKR fee ke sath application den.

3. Time Limit:
Daftar 10 din mein jawab dene ka paband hai.

4. Appeal:
Agar jawab na mile to Information Commission mein appeal karen.

NEXT STEPS:
Simple application likh kar daftar mein jama karwayen.

SOURCE:
Punjab Transparency and Right to Information Act 2013
