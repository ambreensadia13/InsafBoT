SOCIAL WELFARE - BISP AND EHSAAS

1. BISP Kafaalat:
Ghareeb aurton ko har 3 mahine 9000 PKR milte hain.

2. Eligibility:
Ghar ki income 50,000 se kam ho. NADRA data se check hota hai.

3. Registration:
Qareebi BISP tehsil office ya 8171 SMS se check karen.

4. Fraud Se Bachen:
Koi agent fees nahi leta. Sirf official number 0800-26477 par call karen.

NEXT STEPS:
CNIC le kar BISP center jayen ya 8171 par SMS karen.

SOURCE:
BISP Act 2010, Ehsaas Program
