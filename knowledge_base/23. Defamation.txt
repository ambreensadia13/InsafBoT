DEFAMATION LAW - IZZAT KO NUQSAN

1. Kya Hai:
Kisi ke khilaf jhuti baat phailana jis se uski izzat ko nuqsan ho.

2. Civil Case:
Nuqsan ka harjana maangne ke liye Civil Court mein suit.

3. Criminal Case:
PPC 499 ke teht 2 saal qaid ya jurmana bhi ho sakta hai.

4. Proof:
News, social media post, gawah chahiye.

NEXT STEPS:
1. Legal notice bhejwayen.
2. Court mein defamation suit file karen.

SOURCE:
Defamation Ordinance 2002, PPC 1860
