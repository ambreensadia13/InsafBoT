CONTRACT LAW - AGREEMENT TODNA

1. Contract Kya Hai:
2 logon ka written ya zabani agreement jiski qanoon mein value hai.

2. Breach:
Agar koi shart na maane to doosra fariq nuqsan ka dawa kar sakta hai.

3. Remedy:
Court ya to contract pura karwa sakti hai ya nuqsan ka paisa dilwa sakti hai.

4. Proof:
Agreement ki copy, payment proof, email, gawah.

NEXT STEPS:
1. Legal notice bhejen.
2. Civil Court mein "Suit for Damages" file karen.

SOURCE:
Contract Act 1872
