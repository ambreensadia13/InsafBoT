NARCOTICS LAW - CNS ACT 1997

1. Prohibited Items:
Charas, heroin, ice, sharaab rakhna aur bechna jurm hai.

2. Punishment:
Miqdar ke hisaab se 2 saal se death tak saza ho sakti hai.

3. Arrest:
ANF ya Police baghair warrant ke arrest kar sakti hai.

4. Bail:
Bari miqdar mein bail mushkil hai. Wakeel foran zaroori hai.

NEXT STEPS:
Agar phas gaye hain to foran wakeel karen. Khud bayan na den.

SOURCE:
Control of Narcotic Substances Act 1997
