LABOUR LAW - UNPAID SALARY PAKISTAN

1. Right to Salary:
Pakistan Labour Law ke mutabiq har mazdoor ko waqt par tankha milna us ka bunyadi haq hai. Tankha 7 din se zyada late nahi ho sakti.

2. Complaint Kahan Karen:
Aap apne ilaqe ke Labour Court ya Labour Department mein shikayat darj karwa sakte hain. Is ke liye wakeel ki zaroorat nahi.

3. Required Documents:
Salary slip, appointment letter, attendance record, CNIC ki copy.

4. Penalty for Employer:
Court employer ko bakaya tankha + jurmana dene ka hukum de sakti hai.

NEXT STEPS:
1. Pehle employer ko written notice den.
2. Labour Inspector ke paas darkhwast jama karwayen.

SOURCE:
Punjab Industrial Relations Act 2010, Payment of Wages Act
