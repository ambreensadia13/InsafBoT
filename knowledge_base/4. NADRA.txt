NADRA - CNIC AND B-FORM PROCESS

1. Lost CNIC:
Agar CNIC gum ho jaye to sab se pehle police station se gumshudgi ki report len. Phir NADRA center ja kar duplicate CNIC ke liye apply karen. Fees 750-2500 PKR.

2. CNIC Correction:
Naam, address ya date of birth theek karwane ke liye original documents + affidavit chahiye. Processing 15-31 din.

3. B-Form / CRC:
18 saal se kam umar bachon ke liye. Walidain ka CNIC aur bachay ka birth certificate NADRA le jana hoga.

4. Processing Time:
Normal: 31 din.
Urgent: 15 din.
Executive: 7 din.

NEXT STEPS:
Qareebi NADRA Registration Center jayen. Token le kar form fill karen.

SOURCE:
NADRA Ordinance 2000, NADRA Official Website
