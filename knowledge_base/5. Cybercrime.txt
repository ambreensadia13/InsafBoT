CYBERCRIME - PECA 2016 PAKISTAN

1. PECA 2016 - Kya Hai:
Pakistan Electronic Crimes Act online jurm ko cover karta hai. Jaise fraud, blackmail, harassment, fake news.

2. Common Cybercrimes:
Online fraud, credit card scam, social media blackmail, identity theft, pornography.

3. Complaint Kahan Karen:
FIA Cyber Crime Wing. Online: https://complaint.fia.gov.pk
FIA helpline: 9911

4. Evidence Save Karen:
Screenshots, chat, call recording, email sab save kar len. Ye saboot ke tor par kaam ayenge.

NEXT STEPS:
1. Saboot ikatha karen.
2. FIA ki website par online complaint file karen ya qareebi FIA office jayen.

SOURCE:
Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act PECA 2016
