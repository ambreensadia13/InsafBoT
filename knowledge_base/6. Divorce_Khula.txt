FAMILY LAW - DIVORCE AND KHULA PAKISTAN

1. Talaq by Husband:
Mard 3 baar talaq de kar union council ko notice bhejta hai. 90 din baad talaq effective hoti hai. UC reconciliation ki koshish karegi.

2. Khula by Wife:
Aurten Family Court mein khula ka case file kar sakti hain. Haq mehar maaf karna parh sakta hai. Wakeel zaroori hai.

3. Child Custody:
7 saal tak beta aur puberty tak beti maa ke paas rehti hai. Uske baad baap haqdaar hai. Court bache ki behteri dekhegi.

4. Maintenance:
Shohar biwi aur bachon ka kharcha dene ka paband hai. Court monthly kharcha fix kar sakti hai.

NEXT STEPS:
1. Union Council ko notice.
2. Family Court mein case file karen.

SOURCE:
Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961, West Pakistan Family Courts Act 1964
