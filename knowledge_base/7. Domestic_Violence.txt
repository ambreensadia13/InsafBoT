DOMESTIC VIOLENCE - PROTECTION OF WOMEN ACT 2016

1. Kya Shamil Hai:
Maar-peet, gali, economic abuse, ghar se nikalna, bachon se milne na dena. Ye sab domestic violence hai.

2. Protection Order:
Court foran hukum de sakti hai ke mard ghar mein na aaye ya maar-peet na kare. Police madad karegi.

3. Residence Order:
Aurat ko ghar mein rehne ka haq hai chahe ghar shohar ke naam ho.

4. Monetary Relief:
Court mard ko kharcha, nuqsan aur jurmana dene ka hukum de sakti hai.
