BANKING LAW - CHEQUE BOUNCE PAKISTAN

1. Section 489-F PPC:
Agar koi jan boojh kar khali cheque de to 3 saal qaid aur jurmana ho sakta hai.

2. Legal Notice:
Cheque bounce hone ke 15 din ke andar doosre ko legal notice bhejna zaroori hai.

3. Court Case:
Notice ke 30 din baad Magistrate ki court mein criminal case file hota hai.

4. Proof:
Bounced cheque, bank memo, legal notice ki copy saboot hain.

NEXT STEPS:
1. Bank se cheque dishonour slip len.
2. Wakeel se legal notice bhejwayen.

SOURCE:
Pakistan Penal Code 1860, Negotiable Instruments Act
