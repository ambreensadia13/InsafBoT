PROPERTY LAW - FRAUD AND FORGERY

1. Fake Registry:
Agar koi jali registry ya farzi sale deed banaye to PPC 467, 468 ke teht saza hai.

2. Qabza Mafia:
Zabardasti zameen par qabza karna illegal hai. Police aur Anti-Corruption ko shikayat karen.

3. Verification:
Property ki registry Sub-Registrar office aur patwari record se verify karwayen.

4. Civil Suit:
Property wapis lene ke liye Civil Court mein "Suit for Declaration" file hota hai.

NEXT STEPS:
1. FIR darj karwayen.
2. Civil Court mein stay order len.

SOURCE:
Pakistan Penal Code 1860, Transfer of Property Act
